THE GROUND RULES

Terms of use.

A few simple expectations for using Common Ground.

What Common Ground is

Common Ground provides information and peer connection for people in the Philippines. It is not a medical, counseling, or crisis service, and it does not diagnose or treat anyone.

In an emergency

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. To talk to someone now, call the NCMH crisis hotline at 1553. Posts and messages here are not monitored.

Directory information

Directory and group listings are illustrative starting points. A listing is not an endorsement or a guarantee of availability, fees, or quality. Always confirm details directly with the service or group.

Community conduct

  • Be kind and respect different experiences.
  • Do not share anyone’s private or identifying information, including your own.
  • Offer support, not diagnoses or medical advice.
  • Do not post harassment, hate, or content that encourages harm.

Your content

You are responsible for what you post. In this version, posts are stored only in your own browser and can be deleted by you at any time.

No warranty

The website is provided “as is”. We do our best to keep information accurate and helpful, but we cannot promise it is complete, current, or suitable for your situation.

Changes and contact

These terms may change as the website develops. Questions? Use the contact page. See also our privacy notes.
