How we keep it kind

Simple ground rules help everyone feel safe enough to take part.

Protect privacy

Use a nickname. Leave out names, workplaces, phone numbers, and anything that could identify you or someone else.

Be gentle

Respect different experiences. Offer support, avoid diagnoses or harmful advice, and never pressure anyone to share.

Know the limits

Peer support is not professional care. If someone may be in danger, point them to crisis resources.
