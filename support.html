Questions to ask before you book

A real organization is not the same as a verified provider. These questions help you check fit before you commit time or money.

1

What are the fees?

Ask for the cost per session or consultation in pesos, and whether assistance or sliding rates exist.

2

What are the credentials?

Ask about training, licensing, and experience with what you are going through.

3

Is it online, in person, or both?

Check the location, travel time, and what you need for an online session.

4

Which language is used?

Ask whether sessions can be in Filipino, English, or another language that feels natural.

5

Is it accessible to me?

Ask about step-free access, captions, interpreters, or other needs.

6

When is the next opening?

Availability changes often, so confirm the earliest appointment and how to reschedule.
