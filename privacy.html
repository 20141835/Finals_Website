Privacy & data.

Plain-language notes on what this website stores, and where.

What this website stores

If you create an account, the site saves your nickname, your email address, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. If you post in the community, it saves your posts. Browsing the site needs no account and stores nothing about you.

Where it is stored

Everything above is saved in your own browser (using “local storage”). In this version there is no server, so nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and no one else can see your posts. The same also means your account only exists on the device and browser where you created it.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single post with the Delete link under it.
  • Delete your account and all of your posts from the Sign in page while you are signed in.
  • Or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Posting safely

Use a nickname. Leave out full names, workplaces, phone numbers, and anything that could identify you or someone else. This website is not monitored for emergencies; if you are in danger, call 911 or the NCMH crisis hotline at 1553.

Questions

Ask on the contact page.
