CARE, RESPECT & CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Support should feel human. And transparent.

Our approach →

Your story, your choice

Use a nickname and share only what feels comfortable. Public posts may be visible to others — leave out names, contact details, identifying information, and anything that could make you feel less safe.

Privacy & your data →

Kindness, with boundaries

Respect different experiences. Avoid diagnoses, harmful advice, or pressure to share. Report concerning content; peer support is not a substitute for professional care, and crisis situations should be directed to trusted hotlines.

Community guidelines →

Clarity you can count on

A real organization is not the same as a verified provider. We make that distinction visible. Check credentials, fees, access needs, fit, and current availability directly before choosing care — and remember that directory examples are illustrative, not confirmed.

URGENT SUPPORT · PHILIPPINES

You deserve support in this moment.

You don’t need to wait until things feel worse to reach out — and local crisis lines are available for immediate support.

This website is not a crisis service — it is a directory and community space.

Posts and messages are not monitored for emergencies. Please use the crisis resources below, including the NCMH hotline and 911 for immediate danger.

PHILIPPINES · 24/7

1553 NCMH Crisis
Hotline

NCMH · 24/7. Also call 0917-899-8727, 0966-351-4518, or 0919-057-1553. 1553 is listed toll-free via landline in Luzon; mobile charges may vary.

Call 1553 Visit NCMH

Sources: DOH CAR · PIA (Oct 2025)

PHILIPPINES · EMERGENCY

911

If you or someone else is in immediate danger or has a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
