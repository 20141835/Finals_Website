CARE, RESPECT & CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Support should feel human. And transparent.

Common Ground is a website that helps people in the find mental health support, connect with peers, and take the next step at their own pace.

What this project is — and is not

Being clear about limits is part of being trustworthy.

What we offer

Information and connection to help you decide what to do next.

  • A directory of kinds of mental health support, with access cues
  • Peer conversations and support-group concepts
  • Plain-language questions to ask before choosing care
  • Crisis hotlines

What we are not

We cannot replace professional care or emergency services.

  • Not a crisis service — posts are not monitored for emergencies
  • Not a source of diagnosis or treatment
  • Not an endorsement of any provider listed
  • Not a guarantee of availability — please confirm directly

OUR APPROACH

Care, with boundaries.

Your story, your choice

Use a nickname and share only what feels comfortable. Public posts may be visible to others — leave out names, contact details, identifying information, and anything that could make you feel less safe.

Kindness, with boundaries

Respect different experiences. Avoid diagnoses, harmful advice, or pressure to share. Report concerning content; peer support is not a substitute for professional care, and crisis situations should be directed to trusted hotlines.

Clarity you can count on

A real organization is not the same as a verified provider. We make that distinction visible. Check credentials, fees, access needs, fit, and current availability directly before choosing care — and remember that directory examples are illustrative, not confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the things people ask most.

Do I need an account to find help?

No. You can browse services, groups, and conversations without signing in. An account is only needed to post or reply in the community.

Is this a crisis service?

No. Posts and messages are not monitored for emergencies. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911, or call the NCMH crisis hotline at 1553 (24/7).

How is my privacy protected?

Use a nickname and avoid identifying details in public posts. Anything you post publicly may be seen by others, so share only what you are comfortable with.

How can I suggest a resource or ask about accessibility?

Use the contact page. Choose “Suggest a resource” or “Accessibility” so your message reaches the right place.

Accessibility

Everyone should be able to find help here. What we do today:

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Headings, landmarks, and form labels are used so screen readers can navigate each page.
  • Text uses readable sizes and contrast, and the layout adapts from phones to large screens.
  • Animations are switched off if your device is set to reduce motion.

Found something hard to use? Tell us on the contact page and we will work on it.
