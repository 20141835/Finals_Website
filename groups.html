Before you join a group

A good group should leave you feeling safer, not more pressured. These checks help.

1

Who runs it?

Ask who facilitates, and what training or lived experience they have.

2

Who is it for?

Check the eligibility, age range, and whether it focuses on a specific experience.

3

How is privacy handled?

Ask about confidentiality, recordings, and what happens to messages or attendance lists.

4

What are the logistics?

Confirm the schedule, venue or link, language, cost, and accessibility features.

5

Can I just listen first?

Many groups welcome people who observe for a session or two before sharing.

6

What if it is not a fit?

It is okay to leave. A group that does not suit you says nothing about you.
